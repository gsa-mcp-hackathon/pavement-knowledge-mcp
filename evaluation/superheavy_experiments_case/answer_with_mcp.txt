### Relevance to the proposed transport

These experiments support calibration of models for:

- Localized wheel-load response near a shoulder.
- Sensitivity to wheel-to-edge and wheel-to-utility offset.
- Load transfer to representative flexible and rigid utilities.
- Transition from repeated short-duration response toward local failure.

They **do not directly validate** a multiwheel transporter carrying a 3-million-pound transformer. Loading used one circular plate, only 25 cycles per level, and a laboratory pavement with one slope, soil condition, utility depth, and two deliberately selected utility sections. Multiwheel stress overlap, axle spacing, tire/contact pressure, vehicle speed, load sharing, steering/wander, field variability, and transporter tare weight were not reproduced. Moreover, this volume does not present analyzed results or failure loads for Experiments 4 and 5; it refers those results to Volumes VII and VIII (p. 90 and p. 108). The introductory summary mentions three utility loading locations, while the detailed protocol documents two; I cannot resolve that discrepancy from the retrieved report.

### Follow-up validation task

Conduct an instrumented full-scale proof test using a representative multiwheel bogie—or equivalent synchronized actuators—matching the proposed transporter’s verified wheel loads, spacing, tire pressures, and speed. Test critical edge offsets and a representative utility crossing while measuring the same displacement, stress, and utility-strain responses; compare these with calibrated predictions before authorizing movement.
