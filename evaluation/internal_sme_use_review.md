# Team use and SME review

Pavement Knowledge MCP team. Experience recorded November 16, 2026.

Our team and colleagues used Pavement Knowledge MCP for many pavement questions, with answers reviewed by pavement subject-matter experts (SMEs). We found that MCP worked well across these questions.

The two [documented comparisons](README.md) are examples of this broader use, with complete paired answers, source checks and scores.

Broader feedback, including the [Claude trial](claude_trial_review.md), is qualitative. We do not report an overall accuracy percentage.

SME review remains important: the documented sensor case shows that an answer can repeat a technical error found in a source report, even when it cites that report.
